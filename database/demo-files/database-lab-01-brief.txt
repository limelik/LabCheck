LAB 1: DESIGN AND QUERY A CAMPUS LIBRARY DATABASE

Goal
Model a small campus library, load the supplied book catalogue, and answer
practical questions with SQL. Allow approximately 90 minutes.

Preparation
Use MySQL 8 and a separate practice database named campus_library.
The catalogue is fictional. Prices are in AMD.

Tasks
1. Create a books table with book_id (primary key), title, author, category,
   publication_year, available_copies, and price_amd. Choose suitable types
   and enforce non-negative copy counts and prices.
2. Import all 12 records from library-catalogue.csv. Confirm the row count.
3. List available books, ordered by title.
4. Find Computing books published from 2020 onward.
5. Show the number of titles and total available copies per category.
6. Find books with zero available copies and calculate the average price
   per category, rounded to two decimal places.
7. Explain why book_id is a better primary key than title.

Submit
One SQL script containing your CREATE TABLE, INSERT statements and queries;
plus a completed lab-report-template.txt with results and a short reflection.
Present your queries during the lab session.

Assessment (4 points)
Schema and constraints: 1 point. Correct data import: 1 point.
Queries and result accuracy: 1 point. Explanation and report: 1 point.

Self-check
12 titles; 30 available copies; 3 titles have no copies available.
Computing has 5 titles and 13 available copies.
